Quiz

Listen to this page

Ten questions covering the whole tutorial. Choose an answer to see whether it is right and why. Nothing is recorded.

1. Why can a single image not determine absolute depth through geometry alone?

Projection divides by depth, so (X, Y, Z) and (2X, 2Y, 2Z) land on the same pixel. Networks must rely on learned cues instead.

2. A relative depth model tells you that a door is closer than a wall. What does it NOT tell you?

Relative depth is only correct up to an unknown scale and shift, so distances in meters are missing.

3. What does the scale and shift invariant loss used by MiDaS allow?

Each prediction is aligned to the ground truth before computing the error, so datasets with incompatible units can be combined.

4. What was the key data change in Depth Anything V2?

Synthetic labels are precise, and pseudo labeled real images close the gap between synthetic and real appearance.

5. A relative model predicts disparity 0.8 at 1 m and 0.2 at 4 m. Using scale and shift alignment, what is the depth of a pixel with disparity 0.5?

Solving gives s = 1.25 and t = 0. Then 1.25 × 0.5 = 0.625 inverse meters, which is 1.6 m.

6. Glass and mirrors are hard mainly because...

The visible content is far away or virtual, so the model reports a large distance at the surface the user could walk into.

7. Which uncertainty method works on a frozen pretrained model without retraining?

Flipping or relighting the input and measuring disagreement only needs extra forward passes.

8. In the guidance logic, what should happen when uncertainty is above the threshold?

An honest uncertain message avoids confidently wrong guidance and gives the model a better view.

9. Why does the smoothing filter in Part 7 accept a much closer reading immediately?

False alarms are inconvenient, but delayed real warnings are dangerous, so the filter is deliberately asymmetric.

10. Which metric would most directly measure the most dangerous failure for navigation?

Saying the path is clear when it is not is the error most likely to cause harm, so it deserves its own measurement.
